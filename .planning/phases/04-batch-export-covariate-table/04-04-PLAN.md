<objective>
Create `scripts/run_batch_export.py` — the production entry point that runs the whole pipeline across
all 4,841 wards for the full historical range and produces `outputs/covariate_table.csv`.

Purpose: EXPORT-01 and EXPORT-03. This is where the pieces built in plans 04-01 through 04-03 become
an operation someone can actually run. It is also where CONTEXT.md's biggest decisions land: D-01's
full 1991-present backfill, D-02's honest warning that this costs hours and real quota, D-05's ward-batch
chunking with automatic re-concatenation, D-06's CSV destination, and D-09's fallback-ward transparency.

**Read this before assessing completion.** This phase has a deliberate split:

- The **fast test suite** verifies the script's *logic* — chunk partitioning, date validation, the
  coverage gate, CSV concatenation — on small bounded inputs, credential-free, in well under 30 seconds.
- The **production run** — the real 1991-present, 4,841-ward, ~10-25-chunk, hours-long backfill — is a
  separate, deliberate, monitored, one-time operational invocation that happens AFTER this phase's plans
  are verified. It is listed in 04-VALIDATION.md under "Manual-Only Verifications".

No task in this plan has acceptance criteria requiring the full backfill to complete, and
`outputs/covariate_table.csv` at full scale is explicitly NOT a completion criterion for this phase.
The plan-checker and the verifier should treat "the script works correctly on small samples and its
chunk plan for the real asset is sound" as the bar, not "the full CSV exists".

Output: `scripts/run_batch_export.py`, extended `tests/test_export.py`.
</objective>
