<objective>
Fix the remaining two audit-identified issues that live in `heatwave/data/ingest.py` and
`nigeria_heat_index.py`: the fragile per-image `filterDate().first()` join used to match tmean and
dewpoint images (REWORK-02), and the lack of caching around `init_ee()` causing re-authentication on
every Streamlit rerun (REWORK-01). These two files change together because `ingest.py`'s return
shape change directly drives the rewrite of `nigeria_heat_index.py`'s downstream callback — per
01-PATTERNS.md, they form a single logical unit and must not be split across plans. This plan also
carries the import-order fix (REWORK-04's Pitfall 4) that makes Plan 01-01's blessings-stub
relocation fully effective in this specific file.

Per D-01/D-02/D-03, `ingest.py` is restructured around a single multi-band `ee.ImageCollection`
instead of three separately-filtered collections, eliminating the need for any `ee.Join`. Per
D-04/D-05, the `@st.cache_resource` caching wrapper is added only at the Streamlit app layer in
this file — `heatwave/auth.py` itself stays framework-agnostic (Plan 01-01) so `init_ee()` remains
directly callable, uncached, from Phase 4's `scripts/run_batch_export.py`.

Purpose: Eliminate a silent-null-risk join by restructuring around Earth Engine's native
multi-band collection selection, and make the Streamlit app's Earth Engine init idempotent across
reruns.
Output: Restructured `heatwave/data/ingest.py`, fixed `nigeria_heat_index.py`.
</objective>
